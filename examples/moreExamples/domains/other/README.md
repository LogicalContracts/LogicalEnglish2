# Assorted domains

Small models of assorted subjects, each a few rules and a scenario:

- `flying_dragon.le` — dragons that fly, and which ones are happy (a variant of happy_dragon);
- `enclosure.le` — which enclosure keeps a family comfortable;
- `sequencer.le` — designing a groovebox pattern;
- `sunangel.le` — birds and their feathers;
- `augmentedsem.le` — the meanings of a spreadsheet's column labels.

## Start here

- [Flying dragons](flying_dragon.le?scenario=green&query=happy) — a rule "for all cases in which".
- [A step sequencer](sequencer.le?scenario=groovebox&query=design) — is the design playable?

## Try this

1. Open [the flying dragons](flying_dragon.le?scenario=colourless&query=happy) and click **Query**. Only bob is happy: he has no children, so every child of his flies. Alice is not, because her child bob cannot fly.
2. Choose the scenario **green**, where alice is green and flies, and click **Query** again. Now alice is happy too.
3. Click "alice is happy" to see the explanation: every child of alice flies.
4. Open [the step sequencer](sequencer.le?scenario=groovebox&query=design) and click **Query**: "my groovebox is a playable step sequencer".
