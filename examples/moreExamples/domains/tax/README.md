# Australian tax law

Programs from the corpus of the first Logical English (LE1) on the Income Tax
Assessment Act 1997 and on GST (the Goods and Services Tax): capital gains
assets and exemptions, the net asset value test, roll-overs, affiliates,
Division 36 loss sequencing, GST registration and turnover, PAYG
(pay-as-you-go) instalments, small business entities, and a journal's balance.
They are illustrations, not tax advice.

## Start here

- [PAYG instalments](payg.le?scenario=test_quarter_2&query=payable) — the varied amount payable for a quarter.
- [Capital gains tax assets](1_cgt_assets_and_exemptions_3.le?scenario=Colin&query=two) — which assets are exempt.
- [Small business entity](small_business.le?scenario=one&query=one) — the turnover test for a concession.
- [GST turnover](gstturnover.le) — when a business must register for GST.

## Try this

1. Open [PAYG instalments](payg.le?scenario=test_quarter_2&query=payable) and click **Query**. The amount payable for quarter 2 of 2025 is 6250.
2. Click the answer to see how it is worked out from the scenario's figures.
3. Choose the scenario **ato_2_quarter_3**, one of the Australian Taxation Office's own examples, and click **Query**: 11500 is payable for quarter 3.
4. Open [capital gains tax assets](1_cgt_assets_and_exemptions_3.le?scenario=Colin&query=two) and click **Query**: two of Colin's assets are exempt.
5. Open [small business entity](small_business.le?scenario=one&query=one) and click **Query**: Cherrio Charity is a small business entity for 2025, because its expected turnover is under the threshold.
