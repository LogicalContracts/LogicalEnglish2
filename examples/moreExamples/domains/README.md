# Larger programs about a subject

Programs that model one subject each, longer than the programs that teach a
single feature of the language.

## Start here

- [Australian tax law](tax/) — capital gains, GST, PAYG instalments, small businesses.
- [Assorted domains](other/) — dragons, animal enclosures, a step sequencer, birds, spreadsheet labels.

## Try this

1. Open [the PAYG instalments program](tax/payg.le?scenario=test_quarter_2&query=payable) and click **Query**: the amount payable for quarter 2 of 2025 is 6250.
2. Click the answer to see how it is worked out, step by step.
3. Open [the flying dragons](other/flying_dragon.le?scenario=green&query=happy) and click **Query**: bob and alice are both happy.
