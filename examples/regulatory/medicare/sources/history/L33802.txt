=== version 32, revision history date 2021-11-20 ===
Revision Effective Date: 11/20/2021 COVERAGE INDICATIONS, LIMITATIONS, AND/OR MEDICAL NECESSITY: Removed: Criterion III Chronic Lower Back Pain (CLBP) due to coverage expired June 7, 2015 under NCD 160.27 Added: Not reasonable and necessary statement in accordance with NCD 160.27 CODING INFORMATION: Removed: Modifier Q0 under HCPCS Modifiers section APPENDICES: Removed: CLBP clinical trial references 10/7/2021: In accordance with NCD 160.27, coverage of TENS for CLBP is no longer available under Coverage with Evidence Development. Per the NCD, TENS is not reasonable and necessary for the treatment of CLBP under section 1862(a)(1)(A) of the Act. As a result, the DME MACs are removing this requirement as a non-discretionary change to the LCD, per the Program Integrity Manual, Chapter 13, §13.2.4.
