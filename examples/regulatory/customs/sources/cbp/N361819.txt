  Lori Boullet  HERA Consulting  19332 Powder Hill Place NE  Poulsbo, WA 98370  RE:  The tariff classification of men’s and women’s jackets from Guatemala  Dear Ms. Boullet:  In your letter dated May 18, 2026, on behalf of your client, Grundens USA Ltd., you requested a tariff  classification ruling. Your sample will be returned, as requested.  Style 20069 is a men’s jacket from 100% polyester knit fleece fabric. The garment has a full front opening  secured by a zipper that extends to the top of the collar. It features a hood, a zippered pocket on the left chest,  and additional zippered pockets below the waist. The sleeve cuffs and the bottom hem are finished with  elasticized binding tape. The hood, the front panel from the shoulders to the chest, and the back yoke feature  an overlay panel made from a woven fabric consisting of 93% polyester and 7% spandex.  Style 20077 is a women’s jacket constructed from 100% polyester knit fleece fabric.  The garment has a full  front zipper that extends the top of the collar. It features a hood, long sleeves with thumbhole knit cuffs, and  zippered pockets below the waist. The bottom hem is finished with a self-fabric band. Both the front and  back yoke feature overlay panels made from a woven fabric composed of 93% polyester and 7% spandex.  Style 20063 is a men’s jacket constructed from 90% polyester, and 10% spandex knit fabric that is brushed  on the inside surface.  The garment includes a full front opening secured by a zipper that extends to the top of  the collar, a hood, a face gaiter, long sleeves with hemmed cuffs, a zippered pocket on the left chest, zippered  pockets below the waist, and a hemmed bottom.  As noted above, styles 20069, and 20077 are constructed from two different fabrics. It is therefore considered  a composite good, with classification determined by the material that imparts its essential character. General  Rules of Interpretation 3(b), Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), noted. The essential  character of styles 20069, and 20077 are imparted by the knit fleece fabric. 

In your letter, you indicated that for styles 20069 and 20077, both the knit and woven fabrics have been  treated with a durable water repellent finish. Please note that, since these garments are classified under a knit  heading, within the HTSUS, classification in a water-resistant subheading is not applicable.  The applicable subheading for styles 20069, and 20063 will be 6101.30.2010, HTSUS, which provides for  Men’s or boys’, overcoats, carcoats, capes, cloaks, anoraks (including ski-jackets), windbreakers and similar  articles, knitted or crocheted, other than those of heading 6103: Of man-made fibers: Other: Other: Men’s.  The general rate of duty will be 28.2 percent ad valorem.  The applicable subheading for style 20077 will be 6102.30.2010, HTSUS, which provides for Women’s or  girls’, overcoats, carcoats, capes, cloaks, anoraks (including ski-jackets), windbreakers and similar articles,  knitted or crocheted, other than those of heading 6104: Of man-made fibers: Other: Other: Women’s. The  general rate of duty will be 28.2 percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.
