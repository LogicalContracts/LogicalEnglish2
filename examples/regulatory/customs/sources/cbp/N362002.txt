TARIFF NO.: 3926.90.9989
  Gregory J. Derian  Derian Enterprises, Inc.  395 Hasbrouck Boulevard  Oradell, NJ 07649  RE:      The tariff classification of a plastic foam pad from China.  Dear Mr. Derian:  In your letter dated June 2, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  Images were provided in lieu of a sample.  The product under consideration is described as a reticulated polyurethane foam component for incorporation  into your DrainMate™ oil drain container.  It is a single piece of die-cut open-cell reticulated polyurethane  foam that is circular in shape and intended to suppress splashing and agitation of used motor oil as it is  drained from a vehicle into the container.  It is approximately 11 inches in diameter and 3/8 inches in  thickness.   Your submission indicates that this pad will not serve as a filter, is non-replaceable, will not be sold  separately, and will remain permanently incorporated within the finished oil drain container during normal  use.  As this foam component would be considered an article of plastic, and as it is not more specifically provided  for elsewhere, the applicable subheading will be 3926.90.9989, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United  States (HTSUS), which provides for “[o]ther articles of plastics and articles of other materials of headings  3901 to 3914: [o]ther: [o]ther: [o]ther.”  The column one, general rate of duty is 5.3 percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change.  The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other 

duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.
